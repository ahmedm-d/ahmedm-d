### [AArch64 Toolchain & LLVM IR Lifter](https://github.com/tristanhatton/aarch64-lifter)

Collaborated in a team of four at Imperial College London on an ARMv8/AArch64 assembler and emulator in C, extending the toolchain with an LLVM IR lifter, MCJIT execution engine and IR dumper.

The linked repository contains the public lifter extension; the coursework assembler and emulator are not published.
